Brand mark

The mark is the product's own signature, distilled.

VitaePro's whole interface runs on one idea: a small gold mark landing where the AI has something to say. The logo is that same gesture — a single calligraphic stroke forming a V and a checkmark at once, finished with the same dot.

Primary mark

An asymmetric V, weighted like a pen stroke — thick on the down-stroke, tapering on the way up — so it reads as handwritten rather than engineered. It doubles as a checkmark: an approved, offer-ready CV.

The heavy down-strokeGrounds the mark — the weight of a document taken seriously.
The tapering up-strokeReads as a checkmark: reviewed, strengthened, ready.
The gold dotThe same mark used for every AI suggestion in the product — here, applied to the brand itself.

Usage

Do

Keep the gold dot gold, even in reversed or dark layouts. It's the one accent the mark is allowed to keep.

Don't

Don't recolor the dot to match a campaign palette — it breaks the link back to the product's own AI mark.

Do

Drop the container on paper backgrounds — the mark stands fine on its own for letterhead or watermarks.

Don't

Don't flip or rotate the mark — the asymmetry is deliberate and reads as a specific, handwritten gesture.
